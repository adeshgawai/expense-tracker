Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when creating an account, logging expenses, or communicating with us. This may include your name, email address, transaction descriptions, amounts, and dates.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your financial data is used strictly to provide you with insights, summaries, and spending categorization within your Spendly dashboard. We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Spendly uses essential session cookies to maintain your login state and preferences. We do not use third-party tracking cookies for targeted advertising.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data stored with Spendly at any time through your account settings or by contacting our support team.
